      What drives the forecasts

      • Each position compares recent-form and original models with regularized boosting, a linear model and a playing-chance model. Validation chooses history length, blending and small residual corrections.
      • Inputs include short and long form, minutes consistency, attacking output per 90, clean-sheet history, home advantage and opponents' past results. Optional Understat team xG is verified against FPL fixtures and tested with an ablation candidate.
      • Vaastav supplies eight complete historical seasons. Finalized current gameweeks are refreshed directly from FPL. Missing statistics are marked; older scoring eras receive less weight.
      • All performance features use previous gameweeks. Same-GW results, scraped xP and final-season totals are excluded. Historical schedules are final schedules; these tests do not reconstruct every deadline's information.
      • Future weeks reuse current form and availability. There is no model of future injury recovery, automatic substitutions or rivals' future transfers. Rival comparisons retain their optional switch and strict point limits.
