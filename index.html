What drives the forecasts

One gradient-boosted model per position learns from past points, minutes, attacking and defensive statistics, home advantage, and opponents' past results. A recent-form baseline is used if it wins during validation.

  • Historical data comes from Vaastav. That repository no longer supplies weekly updates, so completed current gameweeks are refreshed directly from FPL.
  • Performance features use previous gameweeks only. The scraped xP column, same-gameweek points and final-season totals are excluded.
  • Future weeks reuse the latest observed form and availability. New signings with little history are flagged.
  • Historical fixture lists reflect final schedules. These tests are not a full reconstruction of every deadline's information or your mini-league results.
  • Transfers are compared with holding the squad, after four-point hits. There is no model of future injury recovery, automatic substitutions or rivals' behaviour.
